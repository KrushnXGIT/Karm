stuck on, quick debugging, and generating small test cases or sanity checks.

How to brief:
1. Paste CONTEXT.md.
2. Give the focused, self-contained task (paste the exact failing code + error).
3. Use it to triangulate: if Claude and ChatGPT disagree, that's a flag to dig.
