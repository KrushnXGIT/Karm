# Briefing: Gemini (3.x Pro)

Best leaned on for: very large context (drop in whole papers or the entire
codebase at once), cross-checking math derivations, and multimodal work
(reasoning over architecture diagrams / loss-curve screenshots).

How to brief:
1. Paste CONTEXT.md (+ the relevant source files, since it can hold a lot).
2. Use it as a second opinion on what Claude/ChatGPT produced — ask it to find
   bugs or disagree, not just agree.
3. Good for "explain this paper section and how it maps to our code."

Note: verify the model's current context window and capabilities yourself;
they change frequently.
